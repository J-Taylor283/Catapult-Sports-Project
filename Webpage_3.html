About Catapult Sports

Track Smarter. Train Stronger. Play Better. Meet the people and ideas behind our fictional sports technology business.

The Origin Story

From a campus track idea to data-driven athletic technology.

Catapult Sports was created from a simple idea: What if athletes and coaches could use GPS technology to improve performance?

Our fictional founding team consisted of college students who loved sports and technology. After noticing that coaches struggled to measure player movement and fatigue, they decided to create a GPS tracking system.

Their goal was to help teams understand how far athletes run, how fast they move, and how hard they work during games and practices. Today, our fictional Catapult team is dedicated to helping athletes train smarter using data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding device hardware and platform specs.

1. What does Catapult Sports do?

We use GPS tracking technology to measure athlete speed, distance, and movement during practices and games.

2. Who can use our technology?

Our products are designed for sports teams, coaches, and athletes who want to improve their performance.

3. Can GPS tracking help prevent injuries?

GPS data helps coaches monitor athlete workloads and identify potential fatigue, which can support injury prevention efforts.

4. What sports can use Catapult technology?

Our technology can be used in football, soccer, basketball, rugby, and many other sports.

5. Do athletes have to be professionals?

No! Our fictional business wants to make sports tracking technology available to college, high school, and recreational athletes as well.
